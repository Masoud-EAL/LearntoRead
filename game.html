④ If a player adds up wrong

How to play
  1. Everyone starts at the same time and rolls at their own pace.
  2. Add the two dice to your score, and type your new score.
  3. A wrong answer ends that player's game.
  4. Roll again, or stop and keep your score.
  5. Go over the target and you are out.
  6. The closest score to the target wins — the first to get there breaks a tie.
