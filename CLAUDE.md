# LearntoRead

## Audience — important context

This app is **not for children**. It is for **adult migrants in Australia at
ACSF pre-level (pre-learners)** — adults with little or no prior literacy in
any language, learning to read and write English for the first time.

Implications for design and copy:

- Never use childish framing, mascots, or condescending language. Keep the
  tone respectful and adult.
- Simplicity in UI and instructions is for low literacy, not low age: minimal
  text, clear icons, audio support, large touch targets.
- Fine motor control can be assumed to be adult-typical; difficulty tuning
  (e.g. tracing tolerances) should target learning letter forms, not
  compensating for small hands.
